# 🚚 End-to-End Supply Chain Diagnostic: OTIF Optimization & Margin Leakage Recovery

## 🎯 Executive Summary

An end-to-end diagnostic of **10,000 multi-region logistics operations** revealed a systemic failure in the **First Class shipping mode**, which operates at a critical **46.7% On-Time In-Full (OTIF) rate**. This bottleneck drags global operational performance down to **74.8% OTIF** and generates an annual **Margin Leakage of ~$8.8k USD** through expedited freight surcharges, cancellations, and SLA breach penalties.

By implementing a **two-tier remediation plan**—dynamically adjusting delivery promise windows (2 to 3 days) in high-friction international lanes while auditing 3PL carrier capacity in LATAM and Europe—the operation can:
* **Recover ~$2.75k USD annually** in direct margin (+31.1% of lost leakage).
* **Elevate global OTIF to 85.8%** (+10.97 percentage points) without capital expenditure.
